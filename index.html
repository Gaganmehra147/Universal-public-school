Welcome to UPS Gotegaon

Inspiring Young Minds in Chhota Chhindwara, Gotegaon

Universal Public School (UPS Gotegaon) is dedicated to fostering academic rigor, moral character, and lifelong learning. Located in Chhota Chhindwara, Gotegaon (Narsinghpur Dist., MP), our campus provides a safe, nurturing, and technology-enabled environment.

We blend traditional ethical values with modern educational methodologies to prepare our students to excel in board examinations, competitive entrance exams, and life beyond the classroom.
